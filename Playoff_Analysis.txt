  #1 — Tampa Bay Lightning
  The most complete team in these playoffs. Elite superstar talent up front, the best playoff goalie of his generation in Vasilevskiy, and a veteran core that has been to the Stanley Cup Final three times. They know how to win when it matters most.

  #2 — Colorado Avalanche
  When healthy, Colorado has the best combination of offensive firepower and defensive excellence in the West. MacKinnon is a perennial MVP candidate and Makar is the best defenseman in the world. A legitimate Cup contender every year he's healthy.

  #3 — Edmonton Oilers
  McDavid and Draisaitl are the most dominant forward duo in the sport. Edmonton has the offensive upside to beat anyone in a series. Goaltending remains the question mark that could end their run early or carry them all the way.

  #4 — Dallas Stars
  One of the most well-coached, disciplined teams in the league. Deep roster, elite goaltending in Oettinger, and a system that makes every opponent uncomfortable. When healthy, they're the most complete team in the West.

  #5 — Vegas Golden Knights
  Loaded with veteran talent, playoff experience, and a legitimate superstar in Eichel. They've won a Cup before and the organizational culture is built for this moment. Hill in net gives them a goalie who can steal a series.

  #6 — Carolina Hurricanes
  The best defensive structure in the Eastern Conference. Brind'Amour is arguably the best coach in the playoffs. When healthy, their depth makes them incredibly difficult to beat in a seven-game series.

  #7 — Minnesota Wild
  Kaprizov is one of the most electrifying players in the league and capable of taking over a playoff series on his own. Quinn Hughes on the blue line gives them an elite offensive defenseman. A dangerous team that nobody wants to face in Round 1.

  #8 — Los Angeles Kings
  A veteran, well-structured team with legitimate stars up and down the lineup. Kopitar has been one of the most reliable two-way players in the league for 15 years. A tough out for any team in the West.

  #9 — Ottawa Senators
  Young, fast, and hungry. Stutzle and Tkachuk are legitimate No. 1 players on any team in the league. This group is ready to make noise and the talent is there to upset Carolina if the goaltending holds up.

  #10 — Pittsburgh Penguins
  Sidney Crosby. That's the argument. At 38 he remains one of the greatest playoff performers in hockey history and he elevates everyone around him. This team's window is small but Crosby alone makes them dangerous.

  #11 — Boston Bruins
  A veteran, defensively responsible team anchored by Pastrnak and McAvoy. They'll be organized, hard to play against, and competitive in every game. Likely a Round 1 win, but the ceiling is limited against deeper opponents.

  #12 — Montreal Canadiens
  The most exciting young team in these playoffs. Elite young talent across the roster, a goalie in Dobes who has been outstanding down the stretch, and the most intimidating home building in hockey. If the youth shows up, they'll be must-watch television.

  #13 — Buffalo Sabres
  Thompson and Dahlin are genuine stars. This is a team taking its first playoff steps in over a decade and the experience alone will be valuable. They'll compete but the moment is likely too big too soon.

  #14 — Utah Hockey Club
  A legitimate NHL roster with real talent — Keller, Schmaltz, Guenther, Sergachev, Weegar. Utah is not a pushover. But this is their first playoff appearance ever, and the learning curve against a team like Edmonton is steep.

  #15 — Philadelphia Flyers
  Michkov is one of the most gifted young players in the world and worth watching in every game he plays. The team around him is still developing. This is the beginning of something, not the peak.

  #16 — Anaheim Ducks
  A rebuild going in the right direction with real future stars. The playoffs come too soon for this group, but the experience against Vegas will accelerate their development. One to watch over the next three years.
