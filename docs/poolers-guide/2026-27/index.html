🥅 Goalies
🌟 What is a Sleeper? An under-the-radar player who combines strong points-per-dollar efficiency (proj PTS ÷ √AAV ≥ 25) with a ceiling at least 20 points above their base projection — and not already ranked in the top 30. These are players with a real breakout scenario: a new line, a contract year, a return from injury, or simply a conservative projection. High reward potential, low draft cost. Best targeted in the middle and later rounds.

💣 What is a Bust Alert? An overpaid player ranked in the top 150 who combines High risk — injury history or volatile performance — with poor points-per-dollar efficiency (proj PTS ÷ √AAV < 25) and a tight ceiling of 20 points or less above their base projection. You're spending a premium draft pick on someone unlikely to overdeliver. Proceed with caution.
