🌟 What is a Sleeper? An under-the-radar player who delivers strong points per cap dollar (efficiency ≥ 25) and has a ceiling at least 20 points above their base projection — not already ranked in the top 30. These are players with a real breakout scenario: a new line, a contract year, a return from injury, or simply a projection that feels conservative. High reward potential, low draft cost. Best targeted in the middle and later rounds when everyone else is playing it safe.
💣 What is a Bust Alert? A highly-ranked player (top 120 overall) who combines high risk — injury history or volatile performance — with a tight ceiling of less than 15 points above their base projection. You're paying a premium draft pick for someone with very little room to overdeliver. Proceed with caution.
📈 Top Improvers: Players projected to score significantly more points than last season — ranked by the largest positive delta (2026-27 projection minus 2025-26 actuals). Requires last season stats to be populated.
📉 Regression Candidates: Players projected to score significantly fewer points than last season — ranked by the largest negative delta (2025-26 actuals minus 2026-27 projection). Age, injury history, or weaker team context driving the drop.
