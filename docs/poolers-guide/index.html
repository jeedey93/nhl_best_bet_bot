Methodology
How projections are made
Projections are manually crafted — not generated by an algorithm. Each player is assessed individually using a combination of recent history and forward-looking context.
Foundation
Prior season performance
Last season's GP, G, A, PTS and PPG are the baseline. We weight per-game rate over totals — a player who played 60 games at 1.2 PPG is valued differently from one who played 80 at 0.9.
Context
Team & line situation
Line combos, powerplay deployment, and team offensive upside all influence the ceiling. A player moving to a stronger team or top PP unit gets a bump.
Adjustments
Age curve & contract year
We apply modest adjustments for players entering prime years (24–28) or showing age-related decline. Contract years sometimes produce outlier performances — noted in the scouting notes.
Offseason
Trades, signings & injuries
The guide is updated as the offseason unfolds — trades, free agent signings, and known injury situations are reflected. Team and AAV are kept current throughout the summer.
