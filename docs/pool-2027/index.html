FAQ
Does a shutout count on top of the win? ▼
Yes. A shutout win earns 2 + 3 = 5 pts. The shutout bonus stacks with the win — it's not either/or.
What happens in a tie at the end of the season? ▼
Tiebreaker goes to the team with the lower total cap hit — rewarding smarter cap management. If still tied, the prize is split equally.
What stats are used to score my team? ▼
Your team is scored using 2026–27 regular season stats only. Playoffs don't count. Stats are pulled from the official NHL API.
What if a player gets traded or retires mid-season? ▼
Your roster is locked at submission. If a player gets traded, their stats from their new team still count. If they retire or are placed on LTIR with 0 games, they simply score 0 — no replacement.
Can I change my team after submitting? ▼
Yes — re-submit with the same email address before October 1, 2026 and your previous team is overwritten. After the deadline, picks are locked.
What's the difference between paid and free? ▼
Identical scoring rules. The paid pool ($20 entry) has a cash prize split 60/30/10%. The free pool is just for bragging rights — everyone is on the same leaderboard.
