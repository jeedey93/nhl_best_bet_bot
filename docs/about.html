About Parieur Discipliné

AI-Powered Sports Betting Analysis

🎯 What We Do

Parieur Discipliné is an automated sports betting analysis bot that leverages artificial intelligence to provide disciplined, data-driven predictions for NHL and NBA games. Our mission is to take the emotion out of sports betting and replace it with rigorous analysis and statistical modeling.

Unlike traditional handicapping services, we operate on a fully automated schedule, running predictions twice daily (7am and 3pm Montreal time) to capture line movement opportunities and provide you with the most up-to-date analysis before game time.

🤖 How It Works

📊
Data Collection
We fetch real-time odds from The Odds API and game schedules from official NHL/NBA APIs multiple times per day.
🧠
AI Analysis
Google Gemini AI analyzes historical results, team form, injury reports, and head-to-head matchups to identify value bets.
📈
Line Movement
Our dual-run system (7am & 3pm) compares predictions to identify consensus plays and line movement opportunities.
✅
Results Tracking
Every prediction is tracked and analyzed daily, with full transparency on wins, losses, and unit performance.

📅 Daily Schedule

Morning Run (7:00 AM Montreal Time)

Early predictions based on overnight odds movements. Ideal for getting ahead of the market and identifying early value before lines tighten.

Afternoon Run (3:00 PM Montreal Time)

Updated predictions with line movement analysis. This is when we compare morning and afternoon predictions to identify:

  • Consensus plays that remained strong throughout the day
  • Line movement opportunities where odds have shifted favorably
  • Final "Bet of the Day" selections for both NHL and NBA

Next Morning (6:00 AM Montreal Time)

Results analysis for all previous day's predictions, including performance tracking and unit calculations.

📈 Performance Tracking

We track every single prediction with full transparency. Our homepage displays real-time statistics including: Yesterday's Performance, This Week's Results, All-Time Record, and separate tracking for NHL and NBA picks.

Unit tracking reflects our disciplined betting approach, with confidence levels determining bet sizes:

  • High Confidence: 1.5 units
  • Medium Confidence: 1.0 units
  • Featured Bets of the Day: Up to 1.5 units

All results are calculated based on actual closing odds to ensure accuracy and transparency.
