# New Prompt Quick Reference Card

## Key Philosophy Changes

### OLD Approach (48% win rate)
- ❌ Force 5 picks per day
- ❌ Accept +3% edge plays
- ❌ 8-10 complex adjustment factors
- ❌ Montreal Canadiens forced picks
- ❌ Heavy recency bias (last 5-10 games)
- ❌ Ignore market consensus

### NEW Approach (Target 55%+ win rate)
- ✅ Target 2-4 selective picks per day
- ✅ Require +5% minimum edge
- ✅ 4 clear, weighted factors
- ✅ No forced picks on any team
- ✅ Balance recent + season stats
- ✅ Respect sharp market consensus

## The 4 Factors (Both Sports)

### NHL Priority Order
1. **Goalie Matchup (35%)** - Hot goalie vs cold goalie = biggest edge
2. **Home Ice Advantage (30%)** - Use home/away split gap
3. **Recent Form (20%)** - Goal differential last 10 games
4. **Fatigue (15%)** - Back-to-backs matter most

### NBA Priority Order
1. **Home Court (35%)** - NBA home court is HUGE (.250+ gaps common)
2. **Team Quality (30%)** - Talent gap + motivation (playoff push vs tanking)
3. **Recent Form (20%)** - Point differential + H2H dominance
4. **Fatigue (15%)** - Road back-to-backs are brutal


## What "No Qualified Plays Today" Means

This is **GOOD**, not bad! It means:
- No games have +5% edge over market odds
- Market has priced games efficiently
- Forcing a pick would be -EV (negative expected value)
- Better to pass and wait for tomorrow's edges

**Sharp bettors don't bet every day. They bet when they have an edge.**

## Red Flags to Avoid

🚫 **Don't bet these** (market already knows):
- Team on 5-game win streak (priced in)
- Star player returning from injury (priced in)
- Obvious back-to-back advantage (priced in)
- Recent head-to-head dominance (priced in)

✅ **Do bet these** (potential market inefficiency):
- 4+ books aligned but one outlier at 0.08+ better odds
- Market overreacted to recent variance (hot goalie with bad season stats)
- Tanking team suddenly motivated (playoff opponent coming)
- Subtle fatigue (3-in-4 nights, not obvious B2B)

## Expected Outcomes

**Week 1-2**: Fewer picks (2-3/day instead of 5), might feel "too quiet"
**Week 3-4**: Win rate should climb toward 52-55%
**Month 1**: ROI should turn positive (fewer bets, better quality)
**Month 2+**: Confidence in selectivity = sustainable profit

## Success Metrics to Track

- **Picks per day**: Should average 2-4, not 5
- **Average edge**: Should be 7-10%, not 3-5%
- **High confidence %**: Should be 40-50% of picks, not 20%
- **Win rate**: Target 54-56% over 50+ picks
- **ROI**: Target +5% to +10% (accounting for juice)

## When to Consider Rollback

Only if **ALL** of these are true after 30+ picks:
1. Win rate still below 50%
2. Average edge calculations seem wrong
3. Missing obvious value plays
4. ROI worse than old prompts

Otherwise, trust the process. Selectivity takes discipline.
