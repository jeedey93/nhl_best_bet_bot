PROBABILITY CALCULATION (Simple 4-Step Process)

Step 1: Start with baseline probability
- For home team: Start at 58% (average NBA home court advantage - higher than NHL)
- For road team: Start at 42%

Step 2: Apply Factor 1 (Home Court Advantage) adjustments

Step 3: Apply Factor 2 (Team Quality & Motivation) adjustments

Step 4: Apply Factor 3 (Recent Form & H2H) and Factor 4 (Fatigue) adjustments

Step 5: Calculate Final Probability and Edge
- Sum all adjustments to get your estimated win probability
- Calculate implied probability from odds: (1 / decimal odds) × 100
- Calculate edge: Estimated Probability - Implied Probability
- Only recommend if edge ≥ +5%

Example:
- Base: 58% (home team)
- Factor 1 (Home Court): +6% (.220 home/away gap)
- Factor 2 (Quality): +5% (.180 win % gap, playoff vs tanking team)
- Factor 3 (Form): +3% (+7.5 differential)
- Factor 4 (Fatigue): +5% (opponent on B2B)
- Total: 58% + 6% + 5% + 3% + 5% = 77% estimated probability
- Odds 1.65 = 60.6% implied probability
- Edge: 77% - 60.6% = 16.4% ✓ (Exceeds +5% minimum)

CONFIDENCE TIERS
- High Confidence: Edge ≥ 8% (1.5 units)
- Medium Confidence: Edge 5-7.9% (1 unit)
- Skip: Edge < 5%
