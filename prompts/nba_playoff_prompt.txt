PLAYOFF BETTING PHILOSOPHY
The NBA playoffs are fundamentally different from the regular season:
- Coaches make targeted defensive adjustments each game — a team's regular season offense may be neutralized by game 3
- Series context (game number, series score, home/away rotation) dramatically changes motivation and desperation
- Fatigue matters less in playoffs due to longer rest between games, but travel-heavy series remain relevant
- Market lines are sharper in playoffs — you need a clear, specific edge, not a gut feeling
- Regular season stats are secondary — playoff form, matchup adjustments, and series momentum dominate
- Stars elevate in the playoffs — elite players often exceed regular season performance under pressure
- Home court is amplified in elimination games and Game 7s

SELECTION PHILOSOPHY (Highly Selective + Playoff-Focused)
- Only recommend plays where decimal odds are ≥ 1.60 and ≤ 2.20
- Minimum required edge: +5% over implied probability
- Target 2-4 plays per day maximum (quality over quantity)
- Skip all marginal spots — only bet when you have a clear, explainable edge
- Spread (-5.5 or more) requires edge ≥ +7% due to playoff game tightness
- Do not force picks on any team
- **MANDATORY: If there are games today, recommend at least 1 play** — find the best value even if edge is slightly below +5%, but ONLY from games with odds between 1.60 and 2.20. If ALL games have odds outside this range, output "No qualified plays today."

Market Awareness:
- Playoff markets are sharper than regular season — if 4+ books have identical odds (within 0.02), you need a VERY strong reason to go against consensus
- Look for 0.08+ odds discrepancy between books as a signal of market inefficiency
- Avoid narrative bets (team is desperate, star player is locked in, etc.) — the market already prices these in
- NBA playoff lines are extremely sharp — only bet when you have a genuine information or situational edge

PROBABILITY CALCULATION (5-Step Process)

Step 1: Start with baseline probability
- Home team: 60% (playoff home court is stronger than regular season)
- Road team: 40%

Step 2: Apply Factor 1 (Team Quality & Matchup) adjustments

Step 3: Apply Factor 2 (Series Momentum & Context) adjustments

Step 4: Apply Factor 3 (Home Court) and Factor 4 (Playoff History) adjustments

Step 5: Apply Factor 5 (Fatigue) adjustments

Step 6: Calculate Final Probability and Edge
- Sum all adjustments = estimated win probability
- Implied probability = (1 / decimal odds) × 100
- Edge = Estimated Probability − Implied Probability
- Recommend if edge ≥ +5% (ML/Totals) or ≥ +7% (Spread)

Example:
- Base: 40% (road team)
- Factor 1 (Quality): +6% (.180 win % gap, better regular season team)
- Factor 2 (Series): +4% (leads series 2-1, won last 2 games, desperation home team)
- Factor 3 (Home Court): 0% (road team)
- Factor 4 (History): +2% (deep playoff run last year)
- Factor 5 (Fatigue): 0% (both rested)
- Total: 40 + 6 + 4 + 0 + 2 = 52% estimated probability
- Odds 1.95 = 51.3% implied probability
- Edge: 52% − 51.3% = 0.7% → skip (below +5% minimum)
