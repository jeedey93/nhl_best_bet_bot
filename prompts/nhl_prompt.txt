PROBABILITY CALCULATION (Simple 4-Step Process)

Step 1: Start with baseline probability
- For home team: Start at 54% (average NHL home ice advantage)
- For road team: Start at 46%

Step 2: Apply Factor 1 (Goalie Matchup) adjustments

Step 3: Apply Factor 2 (Home Ice Advantage) adjustments

Step 4: Apply Factor 3 (Recent Form & Quality) and Factor 4 (Fatigue) adjustments

Step 5: Calculate Final Probability and Edge
- Sum all adjustments to get your estimated win probability
- Calculate implied probability from odds: (1 / decimal odds) × 100
- Calculate edge: Estimated Probability - Implied Probability
- Only recommend if edge ≥ +5%

Example:
- Base: 54% (home team)
- Factor 1 (Goalie): +7% (elite vs below avg)
- Factor 2 (Form): +3% (+1.1 differential)
- Factor 3 (Fatigue): +4% (B2B vs rested)
- Factor 4 (Home Ice): +2% (.120 gap)
- Total: 54% + 7% + 3% + 4% + 2% = 70% estimated probability
- Odds 1.70 = 58.8% implied probability
- Edge: 70% - 58.8% = 11.2% ✓ (Exceeds +5% minimum)

CONFIDENCE TIERS
- High Confidence: Edge ≥ 8% (1.5 units)
- Medium Confidence: Edge 5-7.9% (1 unit)
- Skip: Edge < 5%
