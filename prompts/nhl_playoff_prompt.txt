PLAYOFF BETTING PHILOSOPHY
The NHL playoffs are fundamentally different from the regular season:
- Regular season stats are secondary — playoff performance, goaltending, and series momentum dominate
- Favorites are harder to back on puck line (-1.5) — playoff games are tight, grinding affairs
- Series context (game number, series score, home/away) dramatically changes team motivation and desperation
- A goalie stealing games is the single most common reason for upsets — never fade a genuinely hot playoff goalie
- Back-to-back situations are rare in playoffs (longer rest between games) — fatigue matters less unless noted
- Market lines are sharp in playoffs — you need a clear, specific edge, not a gut feeling

Market Awareness:
- Playoff markets are sharper than regular season — if 4+ books have identical odds (within 0.02), you need a VERY strong reason to go against consensus
- Look for 0.08+ odds discrepancy between books as a signal of market inefficiency
- Avoid narrative bets (team is desperate, crowd is loud, etc.) — the market already prices these in

PROBABILITY CALCULATION (5-Step Process)

Step 1: Start with baseline probability
- Home team: 55% (playoff home ice slightly stronger than regular season)
- Road team: 45%

Step 2: Apply Factor 1 (Goalie Matchup) — up to ±12%

Step 3: Apply Factor 2 (Series Momentum & Context) — up to ±7%

Step 4: Apply Factor 3 (Playoff Historical Performance) — up to ±4%

Step 5: Apply Factor 4 (Home Ice) and Factor 5 (Fatigue)

Step 6: Calculate Final Probability and Edge
- Sum all adjustments = estimated win probability
- Implied probability = (1 / decimal odds) × 100
- Edge = Estimated Probability − Implied Probability
- Recommend if edge ≥ +5% (ML/Totals) or ≥ +7% (Puck Line -1.5)

Example:
- Base: 45% (road team)
- Factor 1 (Goalie): +9% (Playoff Elite vs Playoff Struggling)
- Factor 2 (Series): +3% (leads series 2-1, won last 2 games)
- Factor 3 (History): +2% (deep playoff run last year)
- Factor 4 (Home Ice): 0% (road team)
- Factor 5 (Fatigue): 0% (both teams rested)
- Total: 45 + 9 + 3 + 2 = 59% estimated probability
- Odds 1.85 = 54.1% implied probability
- Edge: 59% − 54.1% = 4.9% → borderline, skip unless Montreal rule applies
