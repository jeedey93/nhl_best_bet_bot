PROBABILITY CALCULATION (4-Step Process)

Step 1: Start with baseline probability
- For home team: Start at 57% (average NFL home field advantage)
- For road team: Start at 43%

Step 2: Apply Factor 1 (Home Field Advantage) adjustments

Step 3: Apply Factor 2 (Team Quality & Motivation) adjustments

Step 4: Apply Factor 3 (Recent Form & H2H) and Factor 4 (Rest) adjustments

Step 5: Calculate Final Probability and Edge
- Sum all adjustments to get estimated win probability
- Calculate implied probability from odds: (1 / decimal odds) × 100
- Calculate edge: Estimated Probability - Implied Probability
- Only recommend if edge ≥ +5%

Example:
- Base: 57% (home team)
- Factor 1 (Home Field): +5% (.220 gap)
- Factor 2 (Quality): +6% (.200 win % gap)
- Factor 3 (Form): +3% (+9.5 differential)
- Factor 4 (Rest): +5% (opponent on short week)
- Total: 57% + 5% + 6% + 3% + 5% = 76% estimated probability
- Odds 1.70 = 58.8% implied probability
- Edge: 76% - 58.8% = 17.2% ✓ (Exceeds +5% minimum)

CONFIDENCE TIERS
- High Confidence: Edge ≥ 8% (1.5 units)
- Medium Confidence: Edge 5-7.9% (1 unit)
- Skip: Edge < 5%
